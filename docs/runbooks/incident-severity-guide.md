# Incident severity and tickets

## Severity levels
Critical (SEV1): customer-facing outage or data loss. High (SEV2): a major feature is degraded for many users. Medium (SEV3): partial degradation with a workaround. Low (SEV4): minor issue with little impact.

## When to open a ticket
Open a ticket immediately for critical and high incidents. Include the time window, affected components, the key evidence lines, the suspected cause and the runbook that applies.

## Escalation
Page the on-call engineer for critical and high incidents. Update the ticket as the situation changes and record the final root cause.
